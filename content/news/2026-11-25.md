At least 490,000 Russian soldiers had died in the war in Ukraine by late September 2026, according to death records leaked from Russia’s state statistics agency, Rosstat. The files were taken in a hack by the Belarusian group Cyber Partisans and the Ukrainian group Silent Crow. They hold more than 11 million death records from 2021 to September 2026. BBC Russian counted 490,334 military deaths in the files. The independent outlets Meduza and Mediazona reached about 490,000 in a separate analysis. They also estimate that the real toll passed 700,000 once deaths not yet registered are counted.

Russia does not publish its military losses, so the figures cannot be checked against an official count. But the files matched at least 90% of the soldiers already identified by name from public sources. Novaya Gazeta Europe also counted about 490,000 deaths. iStories found more than 499,000 when less certain cases were included. The records show that war has become the leading cause of death among young Russian men. Poor regions in Siberia carry a far heavier share of the losses than Moscow. Men from Tyva and Zabaykalsky Krai died at many times the rate of people in the capital.

## Why it matters

This is today’s highest-impact story because it is the most complete count yet of what the war has cost Russia, drawn from the government’s own records. The war is now almost five years old, and the losses are still rising. The deaths reach into almost every Russian region and will weigh on the country’s workforce and population for decades. Still unclear is whether the Kremlin will respond to the data, and whether the scale of the losses will weigh on any talks to end the war.
